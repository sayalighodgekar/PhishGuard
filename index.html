    01

    What is phishing?

    Phishing is an attempt to impersonate a trusted sender so a person will reveal information or take an unsafe action.

    02

    Why do people fall for it?

    Urgency, fear, curiosity, and familiarity can make a message feel convincing before it has been carefully checked.

    03

    How do layers reduce risk?

    Filters, reputation checks, browser warnings, and authentication controls can catch different warning signs at different stages.

    04

    Why does awareness matter?

    Technology cannot predict every situation. An informed person provides an essential final layer of judgment.

    05

    Technology and behavior

    Tools reduce exposure, while safe habits guide decisions. Effective security depends on both working together.

        Educational by design

        Simulation without real-world risk

        This project will never collect credentials, contact external services, execute malware, scan networks, or transmit user data. Future interactions will be local, predefined, and clearly labeled as simulations.

        About the platform

        Cybersecurity education through safe interaction

        DualShield turns abstract security concepts into an accessible, controlled learning journey without reproducing harmful behavior.

        ◎

        Purpose

        Demonstrate how phishing uses trust, urgency, and imitation while showing how layered defenses reduce risk.

        ♙

        Educational objectives

        Build recognition skills, encourage deliberate decisions, and connect human awareness with technical safeguards.

        !

        What is phishing?

        A social-engineering technique that impersonates a trusted source to influence a person into taking an unsafe action.

        ◆

        Why awareness matters

        Filters can reduce exposure, but informed users remain an essential decision layer when unexpected messages appear.

        ⌁

        Architecture

        A static presentation layer, an encapsulated in-memory controller, reusable scenario data, and mode-aware educational results.

        ◇

        Technology stack

        Semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3, accessible SVG, and modern vanilla JavaScript—with no backend or external framework.

        Educational disclaimer

        Every identity, message, metric, event, and outcome is fictional. The application never requests credentials, contacts remote services, scans systems, or transmits data.
